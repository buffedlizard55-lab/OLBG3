How to read this board

What is verified

Fixture, opponent, date and time are checked against the competition’s own site (NBA.com, NHL.com, URC) and the link is in the row. Where we could not do that, the row says unverified — we leave the competition blank rather than guessing it from a club name.

What is ours

Prices, handicap lines, totals and picks are produced by our own model (method). They are opinions priced off disclosed inputs, not facts, and not bookmaker prices.
