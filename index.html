How to read this board

What is verified

Fixture, opponent, date and time are checked against the competition’s own site (NBA.com, NHL.com, MLB.com, ATP Tour, WST, CSA, URC, LNR, ICEHL …) and the link is in the row. A row that only has a reputable third-party listing says corroborated; a row with neither says unverified. We leave the competition blank rather than guessing it from a club name, and we never promote a third-party link to “official”.

What is ours

Prices, handicap lines, totals and picks are produced by our own model (method). They are opinions priced off disclosed inputs, not facts, and not bookmaker prices.
