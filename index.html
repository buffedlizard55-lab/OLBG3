EVIDENCE STANDARD

How entries are reviewed

  1. 01
    Confirm the event

    Use an official league, tour, federation or event schedule. Record the event's own source URL.

  2. 02
    Build independent lines

    Store our projected price/line separately from market odds; explain assumptions and timestamp every input.

  3. 03
    Assess markets & pick

    Record markets considered, selection, confidence and the evidence behind each decision.

  4. 04
    Publish only supported claims

    High-confidence prose must be unique, at least 30 words, and link verified form and match-history evidence.
