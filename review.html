What this site is. An independent research project that reads OLBG's public betting-tips listings for 15 sports, originates its own prices for every captured fixture, grades a selection and writes a sourced prediction for the high-confidence ones. It is not affiliated with OLBG and it publishes no guaranteed outcomes. 18+.

What a price here means. "Our price" is our own originated line, not a bookmaker quote and not a promise. "OLBG price" is the number the listing printed when it was captured. Where the two disagree, that disagreement is the opinion being published.
