Do not confuse facts with model estimates

Fixtures are extracted directly from official league JSON feeds. Missing fields are blank. No bookmaker lines, community voting percentages or scraped opinions are presented as our own probabilities.

Prediction release gate

  1. Verify participants, start time, venue, current form and recent results from dated official records.
  2. Train and out-of-time validate a sport-specific model; publish training window, inputs, calibration and uncertainty.
  3. Originate fair prices from calibrated probabilities. These are model estimates, never official facts.
  4. Evaluate market settlement rules, handicap and total distributions; validate each market separately.
  5. Assign low, medium or high confidence only against documented validation thresholds, not a subjective label.
  6. Release high-confidence prose only when supported by linked match-specific evidence, with an explicit selection and at least 30 words. Do not manufacture player news or form.

These gates are not applied in this feed track, which publishes no model and no picks. The board track publishes model lines and picks separately, labelled as model output; see the dashboard and its brief-coverage accounting.
