St Finbarrs v Nemo Rangers

Sunday 25 October 2026 · 16:00 UTC

Selection St Finbarrs to win 1.62 (-161) HIGH

Reasoning published

St Finbarrs to win at 1.62 (-161) is our selection for Sunday's McCarthy Insurance Group Cork Premier Senior Football Championship Final against Nemo Rangers at SuperValu Páirc Uí Chaoimh, a 4.00pm throw-in per Cork GAA's own event page. That page prints Nemo's route as a 1-15 to 0-16 semi-final win over Carrigaline and a 1-20 to 1-17 quarter-final win over Castlehaven, and St Finbarrs' route as a 2-12 to 0-16 semi-final win over Clonakilty plus what it calls a convincing quarter-final victory over Carbery, without a scoreline. OLBG's handicap market carries 14 of 18 tips on Nemo Rangers +1.5, a crowd lean we are taking the other side of. The Barrs are the selection at 1.62, inside the -250 to +10000 window, and the print leaves the Carbery margin unstated rather than filled in.
