TWO MODELS, ONE FIXTURE

Cross-track review: 30 of 129 paired fixtures disagree

The repository carries two independent pipelines. The root board builds its lines from official competition tables and standings; the /olbg/ audit track builds its own from the captured OLBG listing with a shrink plus home-edge model. Where the same OLBG fixture appears on both, they can name different selections. Both versions stay published and both are listed here so the disagreement is visible rather than hidden.

A disagreement is not evidence that either side is wrong, and it is not a settled outcome. Neither number is a bookmaker price. The list exists so a human can review the pair; it is also published as FL-105 on the Flagged for Review page. Rows where one track deliberately withheld a selection (source conflict or a gate) are counted separately and are not treated as disagreements.
