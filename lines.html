Markets we deliberately did not quote

  • Match totals outside rugby union and ice hockey. Not originated: we captured no official scoring baseline for this sport in this pass, so any total would be a number we invented. The field is left empty on purpose and the gap is listed on the Flagged for Review page.
  • Cricket — OLBG does not state the format (T20 / ODI / Test), and a runs line differs by an order of magnitude between them, so a handicap would be invented.
  • Darts — Neither OLBG nor the PDC pages retrieved state the leg format for these matches, so a legs handicap cannot be expressed.
  • Gaelic Football — No official scoring record was retrieved for these club fixtures, so there is no scoring unit to price against.
  • Handicaps on outright and race rows (golf tournaments, the F1 race and championship markets, greyhound races, rugby league futures, the All-Ireland championship): a one-selection market has no opponent to give a start to.

How a line is produced

  1. Official form — where the competition publishes a table, expected score is built from it (points/goals for and against), the two sides are compared, and a projected margin and combined score fall out of that.
  2. Probability implied — where there is no official table, the only input is the public tip share. The win probability is turned into a margin with a disclosed per-sport scale (Tennis 4.5 games, Basketball 13 points, Handball 7 goals, Baseball 3 runs, Volleyball 1.6 sets, Snooker 1.4 frames), and the probability is capped at 85% so a three-tip sample cannot set an extreme line. These rows are labelled consensus-scaled and can never be graded high confidence.
  3. Half-point lines only — every handicap and total is set on a half point, so a push is impossible and the quote is unambiguous.
  4. Price — 5% overround spread proportionally; handicap and total sides are quoted at our standard 1.91 two-way price.
