FL-011 data quality

Tiny tip samples and tied splits

Most rows carry 1–3 tips. Two NBA money lines (IND@NO, DAL@HOU) are exact 2-2 ties and four handball rows have "Tie" as the most-tipped selection. Our model shrinks every split toward even money; these rows cannot grade above LOW on tips alone.

FL-012 method

Prices, handicaps and totals are model output, not market data

OLBG publishes no odds in the retrieved pages. Every price here comes from tools/originate.py (Beta(2,2)-shrunk tip split, home-field logit shift, 5% hold). HIGH picks carry a disclosed 65% probability floor justified only by the official facts quoted in the written prediction.
