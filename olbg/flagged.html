FL-002 high

Kick-off times on OLBG are not labelled with a timezone and are internally inconsistent

OLBG prints only 'Today', 'Tomorrow' or a date plus a clock time, never a timezone. Comparing three sports in the same retrieval: snooker 'John Higgins v Jak Jones Today 08:00' matches the official 12:00 UTC start (i.e. US Eastern); greyhound race '11:14 Kinsley' is listed as 06:14 (also US Eastern); but NHL 'PHI Flyers @ BUF Sabres Tomorrow 00:37' cannot be US Eastern, because the official puck-drop is 21 October 19:30 ET. Listed times are therefore recorded as strings only, and no scheduled start in this dataset is derived from them. Where an official start was verified it is stored separately in the 'start' field.
