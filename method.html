Method & scope

Facts, captured opinions and model estimates are different things. This site labels them separately and never treats a source hostname as factual verification.

1. Discovery is not verification

Public OLBG listing and event URLs are preserved. The hourly collector respects robots.txt and makes at most one public listing request per sport, two at a time. It reads actual linked text, never guesses a match from its URL, does not call disallowed private APIs and does not bypass challenges or invent pagination URLs. New event references remain unverified; their UTC time, competition and betting fields stay blank on Current discovery.

2. Official sporting facts

Recorded fixture confirmations and scoring/ranking inputs live in data/official.json and data/verifications.json, with official source links. Some are historical observations, not independently refreshed by this pass. Only retrieved official evidence may fill a factual field; third parties stay corroborated. The NBA/NHL/MLB connectors refresh their own schedules hourly, keep payload SHA-256 hashes, and show per-source successes and failures. Cached rows are withheld from the current feed.

3. Our originated prices

The root model in scripts/model.py uses disclosed sport-specific heuristics: scoring-rate/normal-margin models for rugby, basketball and handball, a goals/logistic model for hockey, Log5 plus an assumed home adjustment for baseball, and assumed mappings of ranking points, ratings or seeds for some individual sports. Without the relevant inputs it may use a public tip share or leave a market empty.

The separate /olbg/ audit model uses shrunk tip shares and home-logit assumptions, plus explicitly marked editorial prior overrides in tools/originate.py. Those hard-coded probabilities and total preferences are assumptions, not official statistics, trained coefficients or independently validated edges. Different disclosed models can disagree.

Both use decimal = 0.95 / p, a 5% price haircut. In an exhaustive two-way book, the reciprocal probabilities therefore sum to approximately 1.05263 before rounding: about 5.26% overround, not exactly 5%. Fair decimal odds would be 1/p. Single-selection field priors do not establish an exhaustive market book.

5. Confidence and publication

LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH are uncalibrated model evidence grades, not observed win rates. The root rubric scores fixture evidence, stored form inputs and its model preference. HIGH also requires a unique sourced prediction of at least 30 words, an originated price from 1.40 to 101.00 (−250 to +10000 US), and a current official-start release.

No observed bookmaker price was verified, so we cannot certify that a market price is in the requested range or that a bet has positive expected value. Do not confuse filtering out very short model favorites with discovering value.

A release requires a dated observation with an explicitly timezone-qualified exact official clock. No timezone is assigned to bare OLBG labels. “Followed by”, “not before”, session ordering and date-only draws cannot establish an exact start. A HIGH release expires at its scheduled start or 24 hours after the source check, whichever comes first. Passed starts are not final-result claims. A conflict blanks affected model lines and holds the pick for review. Inspect all release decisions →
