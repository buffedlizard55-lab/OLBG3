Method, scope and what we refuse to do

How the OLBG site is structured (reverse-engineered)

listing : https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport_Slug>/<sport_id>
event   : https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport>/<Group>/<Competition>/<Home>_v_<Away>/<sport_id>?event_id=<id>
  • IDs found: Tennis 3, Basketball 4, Cricket 7, Snooker 8, Rugby Union 9, Rugby League 10, Baseball 12, Ice Hockey 13, Darts 15, Handball 20, Volleyball 21, Gaelic Football 25.
  • The slug without an ID is a 404; a wrong ID redirects to the right slug, so the slug is authoritative and the ID is cosmetic.
  • The listing exposes, per event: both sides, date/time, competition (only for some sports), the market, the most-tipped selection, the tip count and the percentage of tips on that selection.

How our lines are made

  1. Rugby union (URC fixtures). Expected points for each side = mean(its points-for per game, opponent’s points-against per game), taken from the official URC table. Home side +3 points. Margin sd 13 points gives win/draw/loss probabilities; totals come from the same expected-points pair; the handicap line is the projected margin rounded to the nearest half point.
  2. Ice hockey (NHL fixtures). Same idea with goals from the official NHL standings, home side +0.15 goals, and a logistic mapping of the goal difference to a 2-way win probability.
  3. Everything else. The only input on the page is OLBG’s consensus tip share, so our probability equals that share, capped at 98% (a 100% share cannot be priced). No total, no handicap — there is no scoring baseline to build one from.
  4. Pricing. Decimal odds = (1 − 5%) / probability, i.e. a 5% overround allocated in proportion to probability.

How outright and race prices are made (the one-selection markets)

A golf tournament, an F1 race, a greyhound race or a season-long championship has one winner and no opposing side, so there is no home/away split and no draw to price. Handicapping it or setting a "match total" would be invention, so we originate the win price only:

  1. p = (wins + a) / (total + k), where the prior Beta(a, b) has mean 1/N and strength k (a = k/N, b = k − k/N). In words: the OLBG tip share is shrunk toward "one chance in a field of N".
  2. Decimal odds = (1 − 0.05) / p, the same 5% overround as the two-sided model.
  3. If OLBG publishes a selection but no tip count (Hero Women’s Indian Open), no probability and no price is originated.

Disclosed assumptions (constants we chose, not measured facts):

  • Rugby union: expected points for a side = mean(its points-for per game, opponent's points-against per game); home side gets +3.0 points; margin sd = 13.0 points. Both constants are disclosed modelling assumptions, not measured facts.
  • Ice hockey: expected goals for a side = mean(its goals-for per game, opponent's goals-against per game); home side gets +0.15 goals; 2-way win probability = logistic(1.0 * goal difference). Modelling assumption.
  • Draw: for 3-way sports with official data the draw band is +/-0.5 points around zero margin.
  • Overround: 5% total, allocated in proportion to the true (model) probabilities.
  • Unverified events: the only available input is the OLBG consensus tip share for one selection, so our probability equals the de-vigged consensus share. These rows are marked basis='consensus' and can never be graded above LOW confidence.

Confidence grading

Three factors, scored 0–6: fixture verified against an official source (+2), official current-form data for both sides (+2), model probability of the pick ≥65% (+2, or +1 if ≥55%). HIGH ≥ 6, MEDIUM 3–5, LOW ≤ 2. A row built only on a public tip share therefore can never be graded above MEDIUM, and in practice lands on LOW.

Current split: HIGH 14 · MEDIUM 45 · LOW 78.

What we will not do

  • We do not infer a competition, a venue, a time zone or a score. If the official source does not say it, the cell stays blank and the row is flagged.
  • We do not write predictions for picks we cannot support with verified form. That is why only 14 of 137 rows carry a written prediction.
  • We do not claim that a row is verified because a source exists for the competition. A row is verified only when the specific fixture appears on that source; see the audit page.
  • We do not present our own prices as facts or as bookmaker odds.
