Method, scope and what we refuse to do

How the OLBG site is structured (reverse-engineered)

listing : https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport_Slug>/<sport_id>
event   : https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport>/<Group>/<Competition>/<Home>_v_<Away>/<sport_id>?event_id=<id>
  • IDs found: Tennis 3, Basketball 4, Golf 5, Cricket 7, Snooker 8, Rugby Union 9, Rugby League 10, Baseball 12, Ice Hockey 13, Motor Racing 14, Darts 15, Handball 20, Volleyball 21, Gaelic Football 25 (excluded), Greyhounds 28 (not harvested).
  • The slug without an ID is a 404; a wrong ID redirects to the right slug, so the slug is authoritative and the ID is cosmetic.
  • The listing exposes, per event: both sides, date/time, competition (only for some sports), the market, the most-tipped selection, the tip count and the percentage of tips on that selection.

How our lines are made

  1. Winner markets. OLBG shows how many of its tipsters back the most-tipped side (e.g. 6/7). We shrink that share with a Beta(2,2) prior ((won+2)/(total+4)) and apply a per-sport home-field logit shift. That is our match-winner probability.
  2. Non-winner markets (totals, handicaps, set betting). There is no winner split, so the line comes from the home-field edge alone, the side named in OLBG’s selection is the side we price, and the row is flagged olbg_row_is_non_winner_market.
  3. Handicap and total. Handicap = −(σ × z) rounded to the half unit using the disclosed per-sport margin σ; totals are per-sport baselines. Outrights and races get no two-sided line.
  4. HIGH picks. The side named in the written prediction is the pick, and its probability is floored at 65% (flag high_confidence_floor_applied_from_official_form). The floor is a choice, disclosed in data/model.md, not a measurement.
  5. Superseded (earlier passes). Rugby union (URC fixtures). Expected points for each side = mean(its points-for per game, opponent’s points-against per game), taken from the official URC table. Home side +3 points. Margin sd 13 points gives win/draw/loss probabilities; totals come from the same expected-points pair; the handicap line is the projected margin rounded to the nearest half point.
  6. Ice hockey (NHL fixtures). Same idea with goals from the official NHL standings, home side +0.15 goals, and a logistic mapping of the goal difference to a 2-way win probability.
  7. Everything else. The only input on the page is OLBG’s consensus tip share, so our probability equals that share, capped at 98% (a 100% share cannot be priced). No total, no handicap — there is no scoring baseline to build one from.
  8. Pricing. Decimal odds = (1 − 5%) / probability, i.e. a 5% overround allocated in proportion to probability.

Disclosed assumptions (constants we chose, not measured facts):

  • Match-winner probability = OLBG tip split shrunk with a Beta(2,2) prior, then a sport-level home-field logit shift (tools/originate.py; constants in data/model.md).
  • Our prices carry a 5% theoretical hold. They are model output, not bookmaker prices and not facts.
  • Handicap = -(sigma x z) rounded to the half unit, where sigma is the disclosed per-sport margin standard deviation; totals are per-sport baselines.
  • HIGH confidence is assigned only where a written prediction cites official current-form facts; such picks are priced at no less than 65% (disclosed floor).
  • OLBG start labels are rendered in US Eastern (UTC-4) for this fetcher; the official UTC time, where confirmed, is in the verification column.

Confidence grading

HIGH: officially verified fixture and official current-form facts for both sides, with a written prediction that cites them. MEDIUM: winner market, at least five OLBG tips, and our probability at least 0.08 from even money. LOW: everything else, including every unverified fixture and every row priced from a non-winner market. The same rule set produces both this board and the /olbg audit section (tools/originate.py; translated by scripts/sync_board.py).

Current split: HIGH 5 · MEDIUM 31 · LOW 142.

What we will not do

  • We do not infer a competition, a venue, a time zone or a score. If the official source does not say it, the cell stays blank and the row is flagged.
  • We do not write predictions for picks we cannot support with verified form. That is why only 5 of 178 rows carry a written prediction.
  • We do not present our own prices as facts or as bookmaker odds.
