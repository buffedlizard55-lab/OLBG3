Does a 70%-plus price exist inside the window?

The brief also asks whether there are picks with more than about a 70% implied win chance inside -250 to +10000 US odds, and to focus there. The window can hold such a pick only in one narrow strip: -250 is 71.43% implied and 70% implied is reached at -233.33, so the whole above-70% region is the 16.7 points of price from -233.33 to -250 (6.7 percentage points of implied chance). Anything shorter than -250 is outside the window by definition. A price there is a short-priced favourite, so the edge is thin; the selections published on this board sit further into the window instead. The count of in-window prices and how many of them exceed 70% is recomputed from the rows on every build and worked through line by line on the OLBG lines method page, section 1b — it is not asserted here.

What would raise the count

Only two things can, and neither can be done by relabelling a row:

  • A new dated official observation for a fixture whose start is still ahead of the publication clock, with the competition naming the fixture and an explicit timezone on the clock. That is what unlocks the release gate.
  • Official current-form data for both sides on a sport that currently has none, so the model can earn the third evidence point instead of being capped at MEDIUM.

Both require outbound access to the competitions' own sites. That access is blocked in this environment (FL-077), so the honest position today is the 63-pick shortfall shown above — published rather than papered over.
