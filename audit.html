7Legacy file claimed verification it does not have

data/master_matches.json holds 76 rows, 71 of them marked 'Verified' and 48 marked 'High' confidence, each with a narrative containing specific figures (for example 'won seven consecutive EuroLeague home fixtures by an average of 11.2 points'). The 'official_source_url' on those rows is a sport homepage such as euroleaguebasketball.net/eurocup/ or atptour.com/en/scores, which cannot support a fixture-level or statistic-level claim. Nothing in this repo publishes that file. It has been relabelled a legacy draft and the claims are listed here for review.

Both documents said '76 fixtures' and '48 detailed predictions'. The published board carries 137 rows and 5 predictions; build_full_database.py is a legacy generator whose output is not rendered anywhere. The documents now state the published figures and point at the data files that produce them.

What this audit does not cover

  • 126 of 137 rows remain unverified. No official page was retrieved for those fixtures in this session, so their competition, venue and start are left blank or published as OLBG’s raw label. They are listed row by row on Flagged for Review; absence of a flag would be a stronger claim than we can support.
  • The tip shares are a snapshot. OLBG recount constantly (see check 5). Re-reading a listing tomorrow will not reproduce these percentages exactly.
  • Pagination. OLBG hides further rows behind a “Load More Tips” control. The board is the first page of each sport, so it is a complete list of what OLBG rendered, not a complete list of everything OLBG holds.
  • Our prices are model output. Every number in the price columns is ours, produced by scripts/model.py and scripts/build_gap_sports.py. They are not market quotes and they are not predictions of fact.
