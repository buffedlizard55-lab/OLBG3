2Every published high-confidence pick was priced shorter than -250

All 15 HIGH picks on the previous build sat between 1.07 and 1.46 decimal, which is -1429 to -217 US: not one of them was inside the requested -250 to +10000 band. The arithmetic is unavoidable under the old rule, which demanded a 65% model probability and therefore a maximum price of 0.95/0.65 = 1.46. The rule now demands 55% and an explicit band check, so 6 picks are HIGH and all 6 are inside the band. The 13 favourites that are now too short to qualify (BKB-11, IHO-01, IHO-02, IHO-03, IHO-05, RGU-01, RGU-04, RGU-05, RGU-06, RGU-07, RGU-10, RGU-11, RGU-13) are relabelled MEDIUM with their prices published, and IHO-06 is MEDIUM because its 21 Oct kick-off has passed. No price was changed to fit the band.

18Freshness clock repaired; source freshness remains a separate limitation

The board previously compared dates against a frozen 22 October 2026 build date. It now uses the actual UTC build date (or an explicit OLBG_AS_OF archive override). This prevents past dates retaining a live label on future builds. It does not refresh OLBG captures, verify same-day kick-offs, or independently validate previously stored source claims. Structural validation of URLs and arithmetic is not source-content verification. The separate /olbg/ track retains its own confidence logic.

33Handicaps are now originated for 83 of the 114 fixtures; totals still only where an official scoring baseline exists

Until this pass a handicap existed only where an official scoring table did (URC, Pro D2, Top 14, NHL, ICEHL, SHL): 22 rows. It is now originated for every priced two-sided fixture in a sport whose scoring unit can be stated — 83 rows — by a disclosed probability-to-margin transform with a per-sport margin scale (Tennis 4.5 games, Basketball 13 points, Handball 7 goals, Baseball 3 runs, Volleyball 1.6 sets, Snooker 1.4 frames), capped at 85% probability. Those lines are labelled consensus-scaled because the input is a public tip share, not official form. Cricket, darts and Gaelic football still carry no handicap, and only rugby union and ice hockey carry a total, because no official scoring baseline was captured for the rest: we left the cell empty instead of inventing a number.

41Our prices are model output, not market prices

Every price on the site is computed by scripts/model.py from either official data or the OLBG tip share, with a 5% overround. They are not bookmaker prices and they are not verified facts. Where the only input is a public tip share, the row is graded LOW and cannot be graded higher.
