Flagged for Review

Everything here is published deliberately, with its source, so it can be checked and challenged. None of it blocks the release; all of it deserves a human eye. 24 findings and 39 unverified rows.

Findings & irregularities

#Finding and what we did about itEvidence
1A published prediction quoted figures that were wrong

Four Pro D2 predictions contained errors that the official LNR pages contradict: Narbonne was called 14th (it is 15th), Beziers 15th (it is 14th), Beziers' 240 goals against was called 'second-worst' (Aurillac's 254 is worse), and a round-6 win at Soyaux-Angouleme was dated to round 7. All four are corrected on the predictions page; the audit page carries the before-and-after.

source
2A row was 'verified' against a betting-preview site

IHO-01 was marked verified with tipsterio.de as its source. That page is a preview, not an official competition or club page, so the row is now unverified and its prediction has been withdrawn. This is the only row in the set whose verification depended on a non-official domain; every other verified row cites a league, club or official stats page.

source
3The captured OLBG snapshot no longer reproduces

Re-reading the baseball listing today gives '24 Oct 01:00' for TB Rays @ MIL Brewers with a featured Game Totals tip, while the captured row records '23 Oct 20:00' and a Money Line tip at 3/6 (50%). Tip counts were always known to move; a five-hour shift in the clock label and a change of featured market were not. The captured labels are published unchanged as a snapshot, and no start time is derived from them.

source
4This build started from a repo that already carried three tracks

Earlier sessions had produced a fixture board, an official-feed pipeline and an OLBG audit track. Nothing was taken on trust: the rows were re-read against OLBG, the high-confidence predictions were re-checked line by line against the official URC table and NHL standings, and the defects found are listed below and on the audit page. Where a document disagreed with the data, the document was corrected.

source
5OLBG start times run 7–10 minutes late

On every fixture where we could compare, OLBG’s displayed start time is later than the official one: NHL 19:37 vs 19:30 EDT (both 21 Oct games); NBA 19:10 vs 19:00, 19:40 vs 19:30, 20:10 vs 20:00 ET. Treat OLBG minute-values as approximate.

source
6OLBG does not state a time zone

Times are shown as bare values (“Today 19:10”). They line up with US Eastern for the North American fixtures but with local/UK time for the URC fixtures, so the displayed zone is inconsistent. We publish the string exactly as shown and label the column “Start (as listed)”.

source
7Competition is blank for most sports

Tennis, cricket, volleyball, darts, snooker, handball, Gaelic football and rugby union listings do not show a competition (the URL group is All_<Sport>/All_Events). We left the column blank rather than infer a league from the team names.

source
8Sport IDs are mandatory and some are stale

/betting-tips/Basketball and /betting-tips/Cricket return 404 without the numeric ID. /Ice_Hockey/11 redirects to 13, /Baseball/5 to 12, /Handball/14 to 20. Any link built with a guessed ID lands on the wrong sport or a 404.

source
9Listings are paginated

Baseball, ice hockey, snooker, Gaelic football and handball pages end in a “Load More Tips” control, so the captured rows are the first page and are not a complete list of that sport’s fixtures. One handball row (FC Barcelona v Montpellier) was cut off mid-record and was therefore dropped rather than reconstructed.

source
10Rugby League has no dated match fixtures

At capture time the Rugby League page listed only outright markets (State of Origin 2027, Super League 2027 Grand Final, NRL 2027 Grand Final). Those three rows are now published as outright markets with an originated price; there is still no dated match to place on the board.

source
11Golf, Motor Racing, Greyhounds and the Gaelic championship had no rows

All four were in scope but absent from the previous board. They are now captured: 10 golf tournaments, 1 F1 race plus 2 drivers' championship markets, 6 greyhound races, 3 rugby league outrights and 1 All-Ireland championship row. Each carries an originated win price built from a disclosed field-size prior; none has a handicap or total, because a one-selection market has neither.

source
12OLBG tip counts move between captures

Genesis Championship read 5/18 at 05:20Z and 6/20 at 07:05Z; the USA Grand Prix read 10/31 (32%) and then 11/32 (34%). Percentages on this site are timestamped snapshots of a live count, not fixed values. Rows added in this pass use the 07:05Z reading and record it in data/gap_sports.json.

source
13A legacy file in this repo claimed verification it does not have

data/master_matches.json (76 rows, produced by build_full_database.py) marks 71 rows ‘Verified’ and 48 ‘High’ confidence, and its narratives quote precise figures (‘seven consecutive EuroLeague home fixtures by an average of 11.2 points’). Its official_source_url values are sport homepages such as euroleaguebasketball.net/eurocup/, which cannot support a fixture-level or statistic-level claim. No builder publishes that file; it is now labelled a legacy draft, its status field no longer says ‘Verified’, and it is listed here so the claim is visible rather than buried.

source
14Documentation quoted numbers the site never had

README.md and COORDINATION.md stated ‘76 fixtures’ and ‘48 detailed human predictions’, and the README listed methodology.html, flagged-review.html and js/dashboard.js as live pages after they had been removed. The published board carries 153 rows and 14 predictions. Both documents now quote the published figures.

15A prediction named a venue the sources dispute

The Leinster v Munster prediction said ‘At the Aviva Stadium’, while the official URC Round 3 record lists Leinster’s home fixture at ‘Laya Arena’ and the row’s venue is blank here for exactly that reason (FL-016). The venue sentence was removed rather than picked from two conflicting names.

source
16Gaelic football rows show no market

OLBG’s Gaelic football listing displays no market name for its selections (the market column is empty on their own page) even though the selections are clearly handicap-style. We left market and competition blank.

source
17Team names differ between OLBG and the official source

OLBG says “Bulls”, “Stormers”, “BUF Sabres”; the official sources say “Vodacom Bulls”, “DHL Stormers”, “Buffalo Sabres”. We map these with an explicit, published alias table in scripts/model.py — name normalisation only, no value is altered.

source
18Totals and handicaps are missing for most sports

A total or a handicap needs a scoring baseline and, for a handicap, two sides. We had official scoring data only for URC and NHL, so only those produce a handicap and a total. The other sports, and every outright/race row, carry none: inventing a number would be worse than leaving the cell empty. This is a deliberate gap, not an oversight.

19OLBG was an hour early on a Finnish volleyball fixture

VLB-01 is listed by OLBG at 10:00 ET, but the Mestaruusliiga’s own match notice gives Puijo Wolley v Oriveden Ponnistus at 18:00 EEST on 21 Oct, which is 11:00 ET. Every other row we could compare converted cleanly to ET, so this looks like a single-row slip rather than a different zone. The official time is what we publish, and the discrepancy is stated in the row’s note.

source
20OLBG had a county final a week late

GAF-04 (Rathgormack v The Nire) is listed by OLBG on 1 Nov at 10:30. Waterford GAA’s fixture list and the Munster GAA calendar both put the Waterford senior football final on Sunday 25 Oct at 14:30 in Fraher Field. The official date is published and the row says OLBG is a week late.

source
21An earlier pass recorded the wrong venue for a Basketball Champions League game

BKB-14 was recorded at ‘OAKA, Athens’. The competition’s own game page (game 136250) lists AEK BC v Windrose Giants Antwerp at SUNEL Arena, Ano Liosia, Athens, on 21 Oct, and 18:00 CEST is 12:00 ET as on OLBG. The venue has been corrected rather than left to stand because it was wrong, not merely unsourced.

source
22Fifteen rows were moved off aggregators and broadcasters onto the competition organiser

Earlier passes verified some rows from Sofascore, AS.com, TNT Sports, tipsterio.de, ORF, Eliteprospects and snooker.org. Fourteen of those now cite the organiser instead: the WST match centre for the six Northern Ireland Open rows, the EHF calendar for HBL-05, HC Pustertal and the Austrian federation’s game centre for IHO-01 and IHO-04, and the Swedish Ice Hockey Association’s schedule for IHO-03. One row (BKB-03, KBL) still cites Sofascore because the league publishes no machine-readable fixture feed; it is named as outstanding debt rather than quietly counted as official.

source
23CRK-05 stays unverified on purpose

Eastern Storm v Tuskers could not be tied to a governing-body fixture list in this pass, and the secondary sources disagree on both date and start by hours. Per the ‘leave blank, don’t infer’ rule the row keeps its blank official fields and sits on this page instead of being guessed into the verified column.

source
24Our prices are model output, not market prices

Every price on the site is computed by scripts/model.py from either official data or the OLBG tip share, with a 5% overround. They are not bookmaker prices and they are not verified facts. Where the only input is a public tip share, the row is graded LOW and cannot be graded higher.
