2FL-002 · OLBG start times are rendered in the viewer's zone and run 7–10 minutes late (data quality)

OLBG labels no time zone. For this fetcher the times are US Eastern (UTC-4): NHL 23:30Z appears as "19:37", NBA 23:00Z as "19:10". European fixtures convert exactly (EHF 18:45 CEST = 12:45; Almaty 19:30 = 10:30). The earlier TSV mixed UK and Eastern times; it is kept for audit, superseded.
Affected: ICH-199897, ICH-199896

11FL-011 · Tiny tip samples and tied splits (data quality)

Most rows carry 1–3 tips. Two NBA money lines (IND@NO, DAL@HOU) are exact 2-2 ties and four handball rows have "Tie" as the most-tipped selection. Our model shrinks every split toward even money; these rows cannot grade above LOW on tips alone.
Affected: BKB-534892, BKB-534891, HAN-12044, HAN-12054, HAN-12048

source
12FL-012 · Prices, handicaps and totals are model output, not market data (method)

OLBG publishes no odds in the retrieved pages. Every price here comes from tools/originate.py (Beta(2,2)-shrunk tip split, home-field logit shift, 5% hold). HIGH picks carry a disclosed 65% probability floor justified only by the official facts quoted in the written prediction.

13FL-013 · Each OLBG list is a server-rendered first page (coverage)

Every sport page ends in a JavaScript "Load More Tips" control the fetcher cannot press. The second harvest found ~40 events the first missed by reading sub-league pages (NBA, European, NHL, Other), and one WTA tie (Fernandez v Sasnovich) present at 05:20Z was no longer rendered at 06:50Z although it has not been played. The list is therefore a sample of OLBG's card, not the whole card.
