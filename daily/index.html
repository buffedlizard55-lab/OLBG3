How the line is made

Our probability is the average of a form reading (official tables and results, shown on each row) and OLBG's printed price with the bookmaker margin removed. Rows with no form reading are market-anchored LOW lines. HIGH means every test passed: two or more official sources, an official start time with a stated time zone, a probability of 58% or more, a fair price between -250 and +10000, OLBG's price not shorter than our fair price, a full write-up, and a start after the publication clock.
