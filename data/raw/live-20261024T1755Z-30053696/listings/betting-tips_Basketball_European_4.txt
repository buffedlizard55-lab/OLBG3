For each game, the basketball betting tips will cover the three popular betting markets:
Money Line
Pointspread
Game Totals
For all three markets, overtime is included. Money Line is a straight shootout, either a home or away win.
Pointspread: you are given a handicap each time (ie -3 and +3), meaning the team has to outscore the handicap. If a team is given a handicap of -3, then they have to win by at least 4 points to win the handicap. Should they win by 3, then the bet would be a 'push' (stake refunded).
The Game Totals is an under or an over bet on a set line. The figure set is the total number of points scored in the game (both teams' scores included). So a line of 216.50 would require at least 217 points for the over to win.
