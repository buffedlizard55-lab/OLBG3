Snooker Winning Margins and Handicap Betting Stats
The stats in this section and the two that follow cover the last ten completed editions of each Triple Crown event: the World Championship, the Masters and the UK Championship. The figures come from OLBG's own database of results and cover the main stages only, from the last 32 at the World Championship and UK Championship and from the last 16 at the Masters. Walkovers are excluded.
Match length shapes margins. Average winning margins range from 3.6 to 4.7 frames at the World Championship, where matches are longer, and from 2.5 to 3.1 frames in the best of 11 rounds at the Masters and UK Championship. A player on a -1.5 frame handicap needs to win by two or more frames, while a player on +1.5 covers by winning or losing by a single frame. A match that does not go to a deciding frame finishes by two or more frames, and 76.1% of Masters and UK Championship matches from the last 16 to the semi-finals finished that way.

Snooker Betting Tips FAQs
Are OLBG's snooker betting tips free?
Yes. Snooker betting tips from OLBG tipsters are free to view on this page, along with the comments behind the selections.
Which snooker tournaments do you provide tips and predictions for?
OLBG tipsters share snooker predictions throughout the season, covering the major tournaments, including the World Championship. The tournament calendar on this page lists UK and international events, with links to our betting previews.
Which snooker betting markets do your tips cover?
Our snooker tips cover four markets: Win Match, Handicap Betting and Frame Betting for individual matches, and Win Tournament for antepost selections. For a fuller explanation of each bet type, see our [href=/bookmakers/articles/snooker-betting-guide] snooker betting guide.
Do you have World Snooker Championship betting tips?
Yes. We provide Win Tournament tips ahead of the World Snooker Championship and tips on individual matches as it progresses. For the draw and form, see our [href=/blogs/world-snooker-championship-preview-and-form] World Snooker Championship preview.
How do I read the snooker tips table?
Each selection shows its market alongside the tips and comments behind it. Experts are OLBG tipsters with high strike rates or current-month or six-month profitability in snooker. Value Rating compares the odds of a selection with its theoretical chance of winning, based on the percentage of tips for it.
How does a frame handicap work in snooker?
A frame handicap gives one player a head start in frames. A player on a -1.5 frame handicap needs to win by two or more frames, while the opponent on +1.5 covers by winning the match or losing by a single frame.
What is the most common final score in a best of 11 snooker match?
From the last 16 to the semi-finals at the Masters and UK Championship, 6-5 is the most common final score at 23.9% of matches. 6-3 and 6-2 follow at 21.1% each, and 6-0 is the rarest at 3.9%. The figures cover the last ten completed editions of each event.
Can I win prizes for my snooker tips?
Approved OLBG tipsters who share snooker tips can win prizes in the monthly Tipster Competition. There are two snooker tables each month: one that rewards tips with comments and one that counts all tips. Each has its own qualifying rules and prize split, shown in the competition section above and on our [href=/tipsters/tables/prizes] prizes page.
