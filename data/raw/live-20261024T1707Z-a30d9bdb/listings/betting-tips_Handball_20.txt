Which handball leagues and competitions do we cover?
Domestic leagues
Our handball tips focus on European club handball, with matches from domestic leagues in countries such as Germany, France, Spain, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Hungary. Both men's and women's fixtures are covered. Which matches carry tips depends on the fixture calendar and where our tipsters are posting, so the selection on the page changes through the season.
European and international competitions
We also provide handball predictions for the EHF Champions League, in both the men's and women's competitions, and for national-team tournaments including the European Championships and the IHF World Championships. These tournaments take place every two years, with the European and World Championships alternating, so there is a major international event to follow most years.
Each match is tipped in the moneyline (3-way), match handicap and points total markets, which are explained in more detail below.
Which handball betting markets do we cover tips for?
Our handball tips are available in three markets: moneyline (3-way), match handicap and points total.
Moneyline (3-way)
This works like the full-time result market in football. There are three options: Home Win, Away Win or Tie (draw).
Handball predictions on this market are settled on regular time only, so any extra time is not included.
If a market is labelled '2-way', the Tie option is removed, and the bet is settled including extra time and, if played, any seven-metre throw shootout.
Match handicap
Match handicap works much like a half-goal handicap in football, similar to Asian handicap. One team is given a deficit to overcome and the other a head start, which evens out the contest and removes the tie.
For example, a handicap prediction of Team A -4.5 means Team A must win by five goals or more for the bet to win. A prediction of Team A +4.5 wins if Team A wins, draws, or loses by four goals or fewer.
Points total
A points total prediction is based on the combined score of both teams, with each goal counting as a point. The bookmaker sets a line, such as 63.5, and the tip is either Over or Under.
Over 63.5 wins if the two teams score 64 or more goals combined, and Under 63.5 wins if they score 63 or fewer. Only regular time counts, so any overtime goals are not included.

Handball Betting Tips FAQ
Where can I find today's handball tips and predictions?
This page lists the handball matches that currently have tips from OLBG tipsters. For each game, you can see the most popular selection, the number of tips and the percentage of tips behind it, plus a Value Rating. Select an event name to see every market for that game and read the tipsters' comments.
Which markets do you provide handball tips for?
Our handball tips cover three markets: moneyline (3-way), match handicap and points total. Moneyline is the match result, match handicap gives one team a head start, and points total is an over/under on the combined goals in the game.
Can a handball match end in a draw?
Yes. Handball matches can finish level in regular time, which is why the 3-way moneyline includes a Tie option. In our analysis of the last five completed editions of the Champions League, European Championships and World Championships, ties made up roughly 5% to 10% of games, depending on the competition.
What does a handball handicap bet mean?
A handicap gives one team a deficit to overcome and the other a head start. For example, a prediction of Team A -4.5 needs Team A to win by five goals or more, while Team A +4.5 wins if Team A wins, draws, or loses by four goals or fewer.
What is the Value Rating on your handball tips?
The Value Rating compares the odds on a selection with the probability implied by the share of tips it has received. It is a guide to potential value, not a prediction that the selection will win.
How often do favourites win in handball?
In our analysis of the last five completed editions of each competition, favourites won roughly 68% to 81% of games with a clear favourite. The rate was highest at the women's World Championship and lowest in the men's Champions League and European Championships. A high win rate does not make favourites good value, as the odds already reflect it.
How many goals are scored in a handball game?
Across the last five completed editions of the Champions League, European Championships and World Championships, the average was roughly 52 to 61 goals per game, with the men's Champions League highest and the women's European Championship lowest. This is useful context when looking at points total lines.
Which handball leagues and competitions do you cover?
We cover domestic club handball from leading European leagues, plus the EHF Champions League and national-team tournaments including the European Championships and IHF World Championships, in both the men's and women's games.
