McCarthy Insurance Group Premier Senior Football Championship Final
25
October
2026
Two of Cork’s leading football clubs will meet at SuperValu Páirc Uí Chaoimh on Sunday, October 25, as Nemo Rangers face St Finbarrs in the McCarthy Insurance Group Premier Senior Football Championship Final.
The city rivals have both come through challenging knockout ties to reach the decider. Nemo Rangers edged past Carrigaline in the semi-final, while St Finbarrs overcame Clonakilty to secure their place in the final.
Road to the Final
Nemo Rangers advanced after a closely contested 1-15 to 0-16 victory over Carrigaline. They had also defeated Castlehaven in the quarter-final, winning 1-20 to 1-17.
St Finbarrs secured their final place with a 2-12 to 0-16 win over Clonakilty. Their quarter-final campaign had seen them record a convincing victory over Carbery.
The meeting of Nemo Rangers and St Finbarrs promises a compelling contest between two clubs with a strong tradition in Cork football, with the Premier Senior Football Championship title to be decided at SuperValu Páirc Uí Chaoimh.
