Main basketball betting markets
The three main markets, which are similar across American sports, are Money Line, Pointspread and Game Totals. All three are covered by our free basketball betting tips and predictions.
Market
What you are betting on
How it works
Money Line
The winner of the game
A straight win bet on the home or away team. Tips on a team to win the game are Money Line predictions.
Pointspread
The winner after a points handicap is applied
Similar to an Asian handicap. A team at -3 must win by at least 4 points to win the bet, while the opposing team at +3 wins the bet unless it loses by 4 or more. A 3-point win is a push, which returns your stake.
Game Totals
The combined points scored by both teams
An over or under bet on a set line. For example, with a line of 216.5, the over needs at least 217 points in total to win.
Overtime usually counts towards settlement for all three markets, but always check your bookmaker's basketball settlement rules before placing a bet.

Basketball picks: singles vs accumulators (parlays)
Our basketball picks can be placed as single bets or combined into an accumulator, known in the US as a parlay. Picks from the Money Line, Pointspread and Game Total markets can all be used in either, and you can combine selections from different leagues, or even different sports, on the same betslip. For NBA selections on their own, see our [href=/betting-tips/Basketball/NBA/4] NBA tips page.
Each approach has advantages and drawbacks, and as with all betting, there is a risk of losing your stake.
Single bets on basketball picks
A single is a straight bet on one selection, such as a Money Line pick to win. Each single is settled on its own, so you receive a return on every winning selection even if others lose. The trade-off is that each extra single needs its own stake, so your total outlay rises with every bet you add.
Parlays (accumulators) on basketball picks
An accumulator combines two or more selections into one bet. The odds of each selection are multiplied together, so the potential return grows with every selection you add. However, every selection must win. If one loses, the whole bet loses, and there is no return; each extra selection makes a winning bet less likely.
To work out the return, take your stake and multiply it by the odds of your first selection, then multiply the result by the odds of the next selection, and so on through the accumulator.

Tips for picking good basketball bets
Basketball produces a large amount of statistics, including team, player, head-to-head and historical figures. They are useful when assessing basketball betting tips because different stats relate to different markets: scoring margins to Money Line and Pointspread bets, and scoring pace to Game Totals (over/under).
When analysing a game, useful starting points include head-to-head records, recent form and player numbers such as points, assists, three-pointers, rebounds, blocks and steals. OLBG's own figures further down this page, such as [href=#how-many-points-are-scored-in-basketball-games] average total points by league and [href=#how-often-do-favourites-win-in-basketball-leagues] how often favourites win in each competition, can also help.
Key basketball stats to consider
Stat
What it shows
Useful for
Points per game differential
A team's average points scored minus points conceded per game. A positive figure means the team outscores its opponents on average, and a negative figure means it is outscored. For example, a team at +6.0 outscores opponents by six points per game on average.
Money Line and Pointspread
Pace (possessions per game)
The average number of possessions a team has per game. When two fast-paced teams meet, there are usually more scoring chances, which can push total points up, although shooting efficiency and defence also matter.

Home and away scoring
In every competition in OLBG's own figures, home teams scored more points on average than away teams, so it is worth checking a team's home and away records separately.

FAQ
Basketball Betting Sites FAQs
Where can I bet on basketball?
You can bet on basketball with any UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) licensed online bookmaker. Most list NBA games throughout the season, along with major European competitions such as the EuroLeague, although the range of leagues and markets varies between bookmakers. Our [href=/bookmakers/articles/best-bookmakers-for-basketball-betting] guide to bookmakers for basketball betting can help you compare them.
How do basketball odds work?
Basketball odds work in the same way as odds for any other sport. In the UK, they are usually shown as fractions or decimals, and many bookmakers let you switch between the two. With decimal odds, multiply your stake by the odds to find your total return, which includes your stake. For example, a £10 stake at decimal odds of 2.50 (6/4 as a fraction) returns £25, a profit of £15. Pointspread and Game Totals bets are priced in the same way as Money Line bets, with the handicap or points line set separately.
How do I choose a basketball betting site?
Compare bookmakers on the things that matter for basketball, such as the range of leagues and markets, Pointspread and Game Totals coverage, the odds on offer and the stats available. Our [href=/bookmakers/articles/best-bookmakers-for-basketball-betting] guide to bookmakers for basketball betting covers these points. Only use bookmakers licensed by the UK Gambling Commission.
Is it easy to make basketball betting predictions?
No betting market is easy, but knowing the teams and how they are performing makes basketball predictions more informed. A Money Line bet is a straight win bet on which team will win. If you expect a high-scoring game, the Game Totals market lets you bet on the combined score being over or under the bookmaker's points line. A total that looks too low or too high can point towards an over or an under. Our basketball tips cover the Money Line, Pointspread and Game Total markets, and our [href=/bookmakers/articles/basketball-betting-advice] basketball betting advice guide explains each one in more detail.
How are basketball betting lines set?
Bookmakers set their own lines, meaning the Pointspread handicap and the Game Totals line. Each bookmaker makes its own assessment, so lines can differ between bookmakers and can move before the game as team news and betting activity change. It is worth checking the line you are getting before you place a bet.
Do basketball bets include overtime?
Overtime is usually included in Money Line, Pointspread and Game Totals bets, but settlement rules vary between bookmakers, so check yours before you place a bet. Bets on part of a game, such as the first half or a single quarter, only cover that period. Overtime is not common, and our margin of victory and overtime table above shows how often games in each league go to extra time.
