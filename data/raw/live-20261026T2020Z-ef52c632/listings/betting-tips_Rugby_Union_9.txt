An accumulator (or acca) combines several selections into one bet, but every leg has to win for it to pay out, so OLBG's tipsters typically back win singles instead.

FAQ
Rugby Union Betting Tips FAQ
Are the Rugby Union tips on OLBG free?
Yes. You can view all of the Rugby Union tips and predictions on this page for free, covering today's and upcoming matches. You must be 18 or over to place a bet.
What Rugby Union betting markets do your tips cover?
Our tipsters post match-winner and handicap selections for individual matches, along with ante-post tips on tournament winners, such as the Six Nations. Each tip shows the market, the selection and the tipster's comments, so you can see the reasoning behind it.
What does a handicap bet mean in Rugby Union?
A handicap gives one team a points head start to even up the contest. For example, a team tipped at +10.5 wins the bet if it wins the match or loses by 10 points or fewer. A team tipped at -8.5 has to win by 9 points or more. The half point means the bet can't be settled as a push.
How often do favourites win in Rugby Union?
Across the Premiership, Top 14, Super Rugby Pacific and United Rugby Championship, the pre-match favourite won between 72.9% and 75.8% of regular-season matches over the last five completed seasons. Underdogs won most of the rest, and around 2% of matches were level after 80 minutes. See our [href=#rugby-union-betting-stats-premiership-top-14-super-rugby-and-urc] Rugby Union betting stats for the full comparison, including how favourites perform by price band. These figures come from OLBG's own database of results and historical match odds.
Can I see past Rugby Union tips?
For recent matches, yes. Use the Show Expired Events button above the tips table to see tips for matches played in the last 24 hours. For older tips, each tipster's profile lists the tips they have made over the last 35 days.
What happens to a Rugby Union bet if the match is drawn or goes to extra time?
It depends on the bookmaker's rules, so always check the terms before you bet. Match winner bets are often settled on the result after 80 minutes, which means a draw loses a bet on either team. Draws are rare, at around 2% to 2.6% of regular-season matches in the Premiership, Top 14, Super Rugby Pacific and URC, but some competitions, including Super Rugby Pacific, use extra time in certain matches.
