An Fhoireann Shinsir
Senior Team: Champions & Contenders
Three Galway senior titles in six seasons and a fifth county final in a row. Under manager Cathal Clancy, this panel has made Maigh Cuilinn one of the most consistent senior football clubs in Connacht, and the 2026 side is going for back-to-back Frank Fox Cups.
2026 run

SF · Maigh Cuilinn 4-26 Annaghdown 0-8

Ár Luachanna
What We Stand For
Everybody should have the chance to be welcomed, to take part fully, to grow and develop, and to be inspired to stay involved with our club for life.
Pobal · Community
The club is the heart of the parish. We play for the people of Maigh Cuilinn and the place we're from.
Deonach · Volunteer-led
Amateur to the core. Every coach, mentor and committee member gives their time for the love of the game.
Teanga & Cultúr · Language & Culture
Proud of our Gaeltacht roots. We promote the Irish language, Gaelic games and our national culture.
Cuimsiú · Where We All Belong
Open to everyone, whatever their age, background or ability. There's a place here for all.
Forbairt · Player Development
From nursery at age five to senior championship, we help every player reach their potential.
Leas · Welfare & Respect
Safeguarding, fair play and respect for players, officials and opponents come first.
