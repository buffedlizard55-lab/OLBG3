Volleyball Scoring Explained
Understanding how volleyball is scored makes it easier to follow the volleyball tips and predictions above, particularly for the Set Score market.
How a volleyball match is won
Volleyball uses rally scoring, so a point is awarded at the end of every rally, whichever team served. A team wins a set by reaching the target score with a lead of at least two points, so a close set can run beyond the target. Most senior matches are played as best of five sets, and the first team to win three sets wins the match.

What this means for volleyball predictions
There are no draws in volleyball, so every match has a winner and Win Match tips are always settled. Because the match ends as soon as one team wins three sets, it can finish in three, four or five sets. Those finishing scores are the outcomes our tipsters choose between in Set Score tips, which are explained in the markets section below.
Volleyball Betting Markets: Win Match and Set Score
OLBG's volleyball tips cover two markets: Win Match and Set Score.
Win Match tips
There are no draws in volleyball, so every match produces a winner. For Win Match volleyball predictions, our tipsters pick either the home or the away team to win.
Set Score tips
For Set Score tips, our tipsters predict the exact score in sets. Matches are best of five (see [href=#volleyball-scoring-explained] how volleyball scoring works), so there are six possible outcomes, shown here as home sets to away sets:

For example, a Set Score tip of 3-1 is a home win by three sets to one.

Volleyball Betting Tips FAQs
Where can I find free volleyball betting tips for today?
Today’s free volleyball tips are listed at the top of this page. Each event shows the most-tipped selection from OLBG’s tipsters, and you can open any event to see how the tips are split and a tipster’s pick with their comment.
Which volleyball markets do OLBG tipsters provide tips for?
OLBG’s volleyball tips cover two markets: Win Match and Set Score. Win Match tips pick the home or away team to win, and Set Score tips predict the exact score in sets.
How does Set Score betting work in volleyball?
Matches are best of five sets, so a Set Score tip predicts the exact result in sets. There are six possible outcomes: 3-0, 3-1 or 3-2 for the home team, and 0-3, 1-3 or 2-3 for the away team.
Can a volleyball match end in a draw?
No. A volleyball match is played until one team wins the required number of sets, so every completed match has a winner. That means there is no draw option in the Win Match market.
How many sets are in a volleyball match?
Most senior matches are best of five sets, so the first team to win three sets wins the match. The first four sets are played to 25 points and a deciding fifth set to 15, with a team needing a two-point lead to win each set.
