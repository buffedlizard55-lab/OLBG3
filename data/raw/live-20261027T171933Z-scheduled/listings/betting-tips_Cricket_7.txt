What Are Cricket's Full Member Nations?
The statistics below cover players from cricket's 12 Full Member nations. Full Members are the countries recognised by the International Cricket Council (ICC) as eligible to play Test cricket, and they also contest One Day Internationals and T20 Internationals. Focusing on these nations keeps the figures centred on the established international game, rather than the many developing sides that also play international fixtures.

Top Performers in International T20s
Who hits the most boundaries? In T20 cricket, boundaries are essential — teams have only twenty overs (120 balls) to set or chase a target, so every four or six counts. Over the past year in international T20s, top of the charts for boundaries is Abhishek Sharma (India), with 221 from 680 balls faced — a boundary on average every 3 balls.
Abhishek Sharma may top the list for boundaries overall, but it is BJ Bennett (Zimbabwe) who has hit the most fours, with 160. For sixes, Abhishek Sharma leads again, clearing the boundary 85 times.

Top Performers in ODIs
Who is the top run scorer in ODI matches? With each team getting a maximum of 50 overs, run-scoring is at a premium, and early partnerships can shape an innings — so expect the leading run scorers to bat towards the top of the order. Over the past year, top of that list is Shubman Gill (India), with 937 runs from 13 innings. That works out at a 93.70 average, a highest score of 223 (Not Out), and 139 boundaries (120 fours and 19 sixes). Closest is V Kohli (India), 9 behind on 928.

FAQ
Cricket Betting Tips FAQs
Are OLBG's cricket betting tips free?
Yes. Every cricket betting tip on OLBG is free. Our tipsters post their selections and analysis for each match, and you can view them without any charge or subscription.
What cricket markets do OLBG's tipsters cover?
Our tipsters cover a range of match markets, including the match winner, top batter and man of the match, as well as series and outright markets for tournaments. Each tip comes with the tipster's reasoning so you can see the thinking behind the selection.
What time are the daily cricket tips posted?
New cricket tips are added throughout the day as our tipsters research upcoming fixtures, so they appear ahead of each match. Check back throughout the day to see the latest selections for that day's cricket.
Can I bet on cricket accumulators with OLBG's tips?
Yes. You can combine several cricket selections into an accumulator by adding each one to your betslip, and the accumulator odds are calculated for you before you head to the bookmaker to place the bet.
What's the difference between match tips and ante-post cricket tips?
Match tips are for a single fixture — for example the winner of a specific game or a market within it. Ante-post cricket tips look further ahead, covering season-long or tournament-long outcomes such as the winner of a series or competition, placed before the event gets underway.
Do OLBG cover T20, ODI and Test cricket tips?
Yes. Our tipsters cover all three international formats — Test matches, One Day Internationals and T20s — along with the major domestic and franchise leagues, from county cricket to the game's biggest T20 competitions.
