2026 postseason schedule, results and how to watch
October 25th, 2026
MLB.com
Share
MLB has announced the complete broadcast schedule for the 2026 World Series presented by Capital One on FOX Sports, FOX One and FOX Deportes.
All World Series games telecast on FOX will be available to MLB.TV subscribers who are authenticated subscribers to the applicable network through a participating pay TV provider. ESPN Radio will also provide live national coverage of all 2026 World Series games. MLB Network will air extensive studio coverage throughout the Fall Classic across the Emmy Award-winning MLB Tonight, the Emmy-nominated MLB Central and MLB Now, and Intentional Talk, MLB Network’s longest-running talk show.
Live Spanish-language game broadcasts will be available on FOX Deportes for all games of the World Series, with Univision also airing Game 1 of the Fall Classic. Spanish-language radio broadcasts for all World Series games will be available on Univision’s TUDN Radio.
The 2026 postseason schedule, which is subject to change, can be viewed in its entirety at MLB.com/postseason.
