Rugby League Tips by Competition
As well as Super League, our Rugby League tipsters provide tips and predictions on the NRL, the RFL Championship, State of Origin and cup competitions such as the Challenge Cup.
The NRL (National Rugby League) is Australia's top-flight professional competition. Expect weekly NRL tips through its regular season and finals series, alongside our other Rugby League tips.
The RFL Championship is the second tier of English rugby league, directly below Super League. Our tipsters provide Championship tips and predictions alongside their Super League and NRL selections, and our Rugby League betting stats compare Championship results with Super League and the NRL, including how often favourites win and how many points matches produce.
State of Origin is the annual three-match series between New South Wales and Queensland, and our tipsters make State of Origin predictions each year.
The Challenge Cup is a knockout competition played across the first half of the year, with the final in late spring or early summer. Super League clubs enter at a later round than the Championship and community clubs, and our tipsters provide Challenge Cup tips throughout the tournament, so check back from the early rounds through to the final. Our [href=/blogs/rugby-league-challenge-cup-preview-trends-analysis] Challenge Cup preview and trends analysis cover the format and past results in more detail.

FAQ
Rugby League Betting Tips FAQ
How are Rugby League bets settled if a match goes to golden point?
Most bookmakers settle Rugby League match betting on the result after 80 minutes, so a match that is level at full time and then goes to golden point extra time is usually settled as a draw in a three-way market. Some two-way markets can include extra time, and the rules differ between bookmakers, so check the market rules before placing a bet.
Golden point is used in competitions such as Super League and the NRL. The stats on this page use the result after 80 minutes, in line with three-way match betting.
What is handicap betting in Rugby League?
Handicap betting gives one team a points start, or the other team a points deficit, before the match begins. The bet is settled on the final score once the handicap is applied, so a team can lose the match but still win the handicap bet if it loses by fewer points than its start.
Handicap markets are popular in Rugby League because winning margins are often large, and our tipsters make handicap tips alongside their match betting selections.
Do favourites and home teams usually win in Rugby League?
Over the last five completed seasons, favourites have won most regular-season matches in Super League, the Championship and the NRL, and home teams have won more often than away teams in all three. Upsets are more common in the NRL than in Super League or the Championship.
You can see the figures, including favourite results by price band, in the Rugby League betting stats section above. Past results do not guarantee future outcomes.
How many points are scored in a typical Rugby League match?
It varies by competition. Over the last five completed seasons, Super League has been the lowest-scoring of the three competitions in our stats, while the Championship has produced the highest scores and the widest winning margins.
Our Rugby League betting stats show how often matches finish in each total points band and by winning margin. Use them as context when weighing up Rugby League tips and predictions, not as a guide to what will happen next.
What is the difference between Super League, the Championship and the NRL?
Super League is the top tier of Rugby League in England and also includes clubs from France. The Championship is the tier below it. The NRL is Australia's top-flight competition and includes a club from New Zealand.
Each competition has its own season calendar, format and play-offs, so Rugby League tips and predictions can look very different from one to the next. The previews and guides above cover each competition in more detail.
