Free Tennis Tips and Predictions Today
How tennis picks are listed
This page lists the most popular free tennis picks on OLBG today. Selections can cover matches and tournaments over the next week or two, so click the 'Events' column heading to sort the tennis predictions by date. Where a match is listed, the selection is for a betting market on that match. Where a tournament is listed, for example 'US Open' or 'US Open Women', the selection is for the tournament winner market. To check the upcoming schedule, see the official [href=https://www.atptour.com/] ATP Tour website for the men's events and the official [href=https://www.wtatennis.com/] WTA Tour website for the women's events. Each pick shows how many tipsters have backed it and how many comments have been posted, and you can click through to read them.
Tennis betting markets we show tips for
Tournament Winner
The most popular pick under a tournament heading is the selection with the most tips to win the tournament outright. Tour events are listed by name, for example 'ATP Rome' or 'WTA Madrid'. Tournament Winner picks can also include each-way selections. Each-way terms vary by bookmaker, but they commonly pay on a player reaching the final, so check the terms before you bet. Click the most popular pick to open the tournament tips page, which shows every selection placed, including the each-way picks.
Win Match
This is a straight bet on a player to win the match, with no other conditions. The most popular pick shown for each match is the player most tipsters have selected to win. Rules on retirements and walkovers vary between bookmakers, so check your bookmaker's tennis betting rules before you place your bet.
Set Betting
These picks show the exact set score a player is tipped to win by, for example, 'Player A to win 2-0' in a best-of-three match or 'Player A to win 3-1' in a best-of-five. The odds are usually longer than for Win Match, but the exact set score must be correct for the bet to win.
Total Games
Bookmakers set a line for the total number of games in a match, for example, 20.5 games. The total is both players' games won added together. You choose 'Over' or 'Under' the line, so picks are headed 'Over' or 'Under' according to whether tipsters expect more or fewer games than the line.
1st Set Winner
This is a straight bet on who wins the first set only. It is sometimes a popular choice for tipsters backing the underdog, as a strong start against a higher-ranked player can pay off even if they go on to lose the match.
Games Won
This is a handicap market in which one player starts with a games advantage or deficit. Bookmakers set the line, for example, 'Player A -4.5', which means Player A has to win by a margin of at least 5 games across the match. If you think they will, the selection is Player A -4.5. If you think the line is too big, the alternative is to back their opponent at +4.5 games.
Tennis odds and Value Ratings
The odds for each selection are shown next to each popular pick. We always recommend looking for value tennis bets, but we know many bettors like to combine short-priced picks in [href=/accumulator-tips] accas. Click the 'Odds' column heading to sort the popular picks from shortest to longest odds, or the reverse.
The 'Value Rating' column shows a rating of up to five stars. It compares the percentage of tips a selection has received with its odds. If the share of tips is higher than the chance implied by the odds, the selection could be a value bet. Click the 'Value Rating' column heading to sort the picks by rating.

Tennis Betting Tips FAQs
Where can I find free tennis tips and predictions today?
This page lists the most popular free tennis tips from OLBG tipsters for ATP Tour, WTA Tour and Grand Slam events, plus lower-tier tournaments and team events such as the United Cup, Davis Cup and Billie Jean King Cup. Click any pick to see every selection placed on that match or tournament, along with tipsters' comments.
Which tennis betting markets do you show tips for?
We show tips for Win Match, 1st Set Winner, Set Betting (the exact set score), Total Games (over or under a line), Games Won (a games handicap) and Tournament Winner for outright bets. Each market is explained in the section on the tennis betting markets we show tips for.
What does the Value Rating on a tennis tip mean?
The Value Rating is a score of up to five stars. It compares the percentage of tips a selection has received with its odds. If the share of tips is higher than the chance implied by the odds, the selection could be a value bet. It is a guide, not a guarantee, and you can click the 'Value Rating' column heading to sort picks by rating.
Which Grand Slam has the most games on average?
In our analysis of the last 10 editions of each Grand Slam, Wimbledon has produced the most games per match on average in both men's and women's singles. The French Open has produced the fewest in the men's event. See the ATP and WTA Grand Slam comparison tables above for the full Total Games breakdown.
How often does the winner of the first set win a Grand Slam match?
In our analysis of the last 10 editions of each Grand Slam, the winner of the opening set has gone on to win roughly three men's matches in four, or four in five at the French Open. In women's matches, which are best of three sets, it is more than four in five. This is useful context for the 1st Set Winner and Win Match markets.
What happens to my tennis bet if a player retires or withdraws?
Rules vary between bookmakers. Many settle Win Match bets on the official result if a player retires once the match has started, but bets are often void if a player withdraws or walks over before play begins. Always check your chosen bookmaker's tennis betting rules before you place a bet.
