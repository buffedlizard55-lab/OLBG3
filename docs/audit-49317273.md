## 4. Irregularities found and **not** silently fixed

1. **OLBG's own label and its `startDate` attribute disagree for some sports.** For today's cricket
   (`188838` Pakistan v England) and tennis (`902207` etc.) the printed UK label and the machine
   attribute are the same clock, while for NHL/rugby/basketball the label is one hour ahead (BST).
   The rows are published as captured, the official source supplies the clock, and no conversion is
   inferred. Reproduce: compare columns `date_label`/`time_label` with `start_utc` in
   `data/raw/olbg-harvest-20261024T1755Z-30053696.tsv`.
2. **Cricket `188838` Pakistan v England has two conflicting candidate clocks.** The committed row
   carries `25 Oct 04:00`; the 17:55Z capture carries `Tomorrow 08:00` (`2026-10-25T08:00Z`). The
   ICC match page (`icc-cricket.com/matches/274145`) renders only "04:00 (Your time)" — a
   viewer-local clock with no zone — and the ECB line quoted on the stale sibling branch
   ("8.00am GMT") would be 07:00Z, not 08:00Z, because the UK is on BST on 25 October. **No cricket
   pick is published from this fixture** until an official page prints a zoned clock.
3. **Tennis is blocked at the data layer, not by a lack of fixtures.** Six upcoming OLBG tennis
   markets exist (`902171`, `902172`, `902173`, `902175`, `902207`, `902251`). The repo's tennis
   pricing table `ATP_WTA_Oct2026_rankings` holds **zero players**, and both official ranking pages
   render their tables client-side — `atptour.com/en/rankings/singles` returned navigation chrome and
   date dropdowns across two chunks, `wtatennis.com/rankings/singles` returned notifications only.
   No ranking points were estimated.
4. **Darts is blocked by a consent wall.** `pdc.tv/rankings` and
   `pdc.tv/tournaments/machineseeker-european-championship` both returned the CookieYes consent
   dialogue (13 chunks, no fixture content). The European Championship quarter-final
   `32407 Price v Bunting` therefore has no official clock and stays off the board.
5. **Rugby Union's only upcoming OLBG fixture still cannot be certified to UTC.** LNR prints
   "dimanche 25 octobre — 21h05" for La Rochelle v Bordeaux with **no zone label** on the calendar
   and on the match sheet. Under this project's existing rule (FL-148: never infer a zone from a
   venue) the row is date-only, the release gate refuses to certify it, and the pick is withheld
   rather than promoted. That is why Rugby Union still shows zero live picks despite a fully
   verified fixture, table and scoreline.
6. **Two candidates were withheld as indefensible, not published.** The consensus-driven board
   produced "Kolstad to win 1.71" (`12132`) and "Partizan to win 1.71" (`12128`). The official EHF
   table has Kolstad 0-5 (143/178) and Partizan 0-1-4 (148/184). Both were left at low confidence
   with no written piece; publishing them would have been a pick with no analysis behind it.
7. **The OLBG consensus on `12136` was a tie.** The row carries
   `non_win_or_tie_consensus_mapped`, which is visible in `data/lines.json`. The selection is argued
   from the official group table (5-0 v 2-3), not from the tipster split.
