How this works

Method, rules and every source used

1. Capture

Each day a bounded, robots-aware read of OLBG's public betting-tips listings for the 15 in-scope sports is captured verbatim: HTTP status, byte count, hash and the served HTML. Fixtures, the printed clock, the card's own machine-readable start attribute, the market, the consensus selection and the price are copied out of that HTML. Nothing is inferred from a slug or a neighbouring card.

2. Originate a line

For every captured fixture we originate a money line from a shrunk version of the captured consensus with a per-sport home edge, then derive a handicap and a total from the same distribution. These are our own numbers, published next to OLBG's printed price so the disagreement is visible.

3. Grade the pick

HIGH requires all four: an official, timezone-qualified start that is checked and still ahead; a price strictly inside -250 to +10000; a win probability below 70% so heavy chalk is never dressed up as insight; and a written prediction of at least 30 words whose facts are linked.

MEDIUM and LOW are published too, with the reason for the lower grade printed beside them. A sport with nothing meeting the bar says so on the summary page.

4. What is deliberately not done

  • No value is filled in from a similar fixture, a previous season or an assumption.
  • No clock is converted from an unlabelled local time; an unqualified time stays unverified.
  • No injury is mentioned unless an official league, club or governing-body source states it.
  • No outcome is presented as certain, and no past result is inferred from a clock.

What this site is. An independent research project that reads OLBG's public betting-tips listings for 15 sports, originates its own prices for every captured fixture, grades a selection and writes a sourced prediction for the high-confidence ones. It is not affiliated with OLBG and it publishes no guaranteed outcomes. 18+.

What a price here means. "Our price" is our own originated line, not a bookmaker quote and not a promise. "OLBG price" is the number the listing printed when it was captured. Where the two disagree, that disagreement is the opinion being published.
